Obedience

True obedience is about training a mindset, not just a set of behaviours.

My goal is to empower dogs (and owners) with:

  • A calm and composed mind that can make good decisions
  • Compliance and cooperation rooted in respect for authority
  • Mental resilience and an improved ability to handle stress

To achieve this, I use targeted exercises such as structured walks, duration work, positions and impulse control. These create the foundation necessary for real-world success.

Protection

Effective protection requires good nerves, strong defence, natural protective instincts, vigilance, courage and mental toughness.

This is what we aim to cultivate — as far as the dog’s genetics will allow.

The training emphasises:

  • Strong defence
  • Civil orientation
  • Building confidence and real intent

This approach maximises the dog’s innate potential and effectiveness.
